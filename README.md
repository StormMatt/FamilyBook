# FamilyBook

**Tell us about your child. We'll give them a voice.**

FamilyBook is an AI-powered AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) system that replaces hours of manual programming with a single conversation. A parent talks naturally about their child — what they love, where they go, who matters to them — and FamilyBook generates a fully personalized communication system in minutes.

---

## The Problem

A parent of a non-verbal child downloads an AAC app. They're confronted with a grid of generic clip-art — a "house" that isn't their house, a "cup" that isn't their cup. They must manually program hundreds of buttons, organize vocabulary into folders, add custom pictures, and learn a complicated interface. Up to **60% of AAC users abandon their devices within a year** — not because AAC doesn't work, but because the tools are exhausting to set up and maintain.

Meanwhile, the child sees a wall of unfamiliar symbols and has no way to talk about what actually matters to them — their favorite show, their teacher's name, the fact that the playground is too loud today.

## The Solution

FamilyBook flips the model. Instead of forcing parents to become AAC programmers, it asks one question:

> "Tell me about your child."

From a 10-minute conversation, FamilyBook generates:

- **A visual timeline** of the child's day and week — familiar, predictable, navigable — with the right vocabulary surfaced at the right time
- **Personalized boards** with real photos, real names, real places — not generic clip-art
- **Communication with agency** — the child can demand, refuse, negotiate, and express frustration, not just make polite requests
- **Context-aware vocabulary** that shifts based on time, location, and situation
- **Progress tracking** that shows parents and therapists exactly how communication is growing

Every part of FamilyBook can be personalized by talking to it. No settings menus, no programming interfaces — just conversation.

## How It Works

### 1. Conversational Onboarding
Your personal AAC setup manager walks you through what your child likes to **eat**, **do**, **hear**, and **see**. Every section is skippable. Talk or type naturally — the AI extracts what it needs to build a personalized system.

### 2. Visual Day/Week Timeline
Children thrive on predictability. FamilyBook shows the day as a visual timeline they already understand — morning routine, school, park, dinner, bath, bed — with the right communication tools attached to each part of the day.

### 3. Real Communication, Real Agency
This isn't a polite request machine. FamilyBook gives children the vocabulary for the full range of human expression:
- Demanding: "I want that NOW"
- Refusing: "NO. Stop. Don't want it."
- Negotiating: "Five more minutes"
- Expressing: "I'm mad because..."
- Commenting: "That's funny" / "Look at that"

### 4. AI That Learns
The system adapts over time — learning which words are actually used, adjusting layouts for motor patterns, scaffolding toward longer utterances, and suggesting new vocabulary as interests evolve. AI suggests; parents decide.

## Key Research

This project is grounded in peer-reviewed research:

- **AACessTalk** (CHI 2025 Best Paper) — LLM-mediated AAC with contextual vocabulary cards drove high engagement across 11 child-parent dyads, with parents incorporating AI guidance 78% of the time
- **QuickPic AAC** — AI-generated topic-specific communication boards from photographs, with high SLP satisfaction
- **Temple University RERC** ($5M federal funding) — Context-aware AAC using computer vision, actively in development
- **Abandonment research** — 60% abandonment driven by poor fit, lack of training, and lack of support — exactly what conversational AI can solve

## Impact

- **400M+ people** worldwide have speech/communication disorders (WHO)
- **25-50% of autistic children** are minimally verbal
- Current tools cost **$250-$7,000+** and require professional setup
- FamilyBook requires: **a smartphone + a 10-minute conversation**

AAC does not inhibit speech development — research consistently shows it **facilitates** it.
